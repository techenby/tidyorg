# Members

Members is a directory of the people your organization works with: volunteers, clients, donors, or whoever you need to keep track of. A member is a record, not an account. Members cannot sign in, and inviting someone to your organization does not add them here.

An organization owner or admin turns Members on under **Settings → Organizations → Modules**. Turning it off hides it and keeps every record.

## Who can do what

| | Member role | Admin | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| View and search the directory | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Add, edit, and remove members | No | Yes | Yes |

## Add a member

1. Open **Members** from the sidebar.
2. Select **Add member**.
3. Enter a name. Email, phone, the date they joined, and notes are optional.
4. Select **Add**.

## Find and change members

The search field matches names and email addresses. A member's menu offers **Edit** and **Remove**. Removing a member deletes their record permanently.

## Used by other modules

When Inventory is also turned on, items can be checked out to members. Removing a member does not check their items in; those items show as checked out to someone no longer in the directory until they are checked in.

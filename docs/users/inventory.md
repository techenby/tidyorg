# Inventory

Inventory records what your organization owns and where it is kept. Entries nest inside one another, giving paths such as **Storage room → Blue bin → Extension cord**.

An organization owner or admin turns Inventory on under **Settings → Organizations → Modules**. Turning it off hides it and keeps everything already recorded.

## Who can do what

| | Member | Admin | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Browse, search, and open QR codes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Add, edit, duplicate, and move entries | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Restore deleted entries | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Check entries out and in | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Delete entries, and delete them forever | No | Yes | Yes |

## Entry types

Every entry has one of three types:

- **Location** for a building, room, closet, or shelf.
- **Bin** for a box, tote, drawer, or other container.
- **Item** for the thing being stored.

The type gives the entry its icon and can be used as a filter. The **Parent** field decides where the entry sits in the hierarchy.

## Add an entry

1. Open **Inventory** from the sidebar.
2. Browse into the location or bin where the entry belongs, if there is one.
3. Select **Add item**.
4. Enter a name and choose its type.
5. Confirm the parent, or choose **None (top level)**.
6. Optionally add a JPG or PNG photo of up to 5 MB.
7. Under **Details**, add any labelled facts you want to keep, such as a serial number, purchase date, or funding source.
8. Select **Create**.

Select an entry's name to open it. An entry that holds other entries opens as a list of its contents; an empty one opens its own page. The breadcrumbs and the back button move up the hierarchy.

## View an entry

An entry's page shows its type, where it is, its photo, its details, and its contents. **Add item** on that page adds something inside it. **View** in an entry's menu opens the page from the list.

## Search and filter

The search field finds entries by name anywhere in the inventory and shows what each result is inside. The filter menu limits the list to Locations, Bins, or Items, and can show deleted entries instead.

## Change entries

An entry's menu offers:

- **QR code**: a code and link that open the entry. Print it and stick it on the bin or shelf. Scanning it asks the person to sign in, and only works for members of your organization.
- **Edit**: change the name, photo, type, parent, or details.
- **Duplicate**: make up to 25 copies, for sets of matching bins or equipment.
- **Move to organization**: hand the entry to another organization you belong to that also uses Inventory. Anything inside the entry stays behind at the top level.
- **Delete**: move the entry to the deleted list. Its contents move to the top level.

Tick several entries to change their parent or delete them together.

## Check items out

When your organization also uses **Members**, an entry's page shows whether it is available. **Check out** records which member has it and when; **Check in** records that it is back. Checked-out entries carry the person's name in the inventory list.

Without Members this section does not appear. If Members is turned off later, check-out records are kept and return when it is turned back on.

## Deleted entries

Choose **Show deleted** in the filter menu to see deleted entries. **Restore** puts one back. **Delete forever** removes it and its photo permanently and cannot be undone.
